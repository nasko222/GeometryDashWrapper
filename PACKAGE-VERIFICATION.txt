Runtime limitation
------------------
The real Windows x64 executable was not compiled or run in this Linux packaging
environment. The raw library has therefore not yet executed its constructors or
JNI_OnLoad through the real Dynarmic JIT. A complete 2.2 beta APK was not supplied,
so nativeSetApkPath, nativeInit, Java/JNI behavior, assets, rendering, input, audio,
and gameplay remain bring-up targets rather than confirmed working features.
