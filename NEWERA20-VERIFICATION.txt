GeometryDashWrapper 0.9.7-newera20 verification
===============================================

Base / rollback
---------------
- Source was rebuilt from the exact 0.9.7-newera15 full-source tree.
- x86 and legacy ARM proxy-transform functions are byte-for-byte unchanged from newera15.
- Camera BASE equations remain newera15:
  * cube X: min(0, 120 - playerX)
  * cube Y: hidden PlayLayer game-layer Y only (never PlayerObject Y)
  * ship/ball/UFO Y: hidden PlayLayer CCCamera Y, with the same bounded-area fallback

Play-only camera zoom
---------------------
- Playtest scale is absolute 0.90 on both editor game layer and wrapper proxy/trail root.
- Saved editor magnifier scale is NOT referenced by the playtest camera function.
- Stop restores the exact saved editor X/Y and scale X/Y.
- Horizontal compensation preserves the newera15 player screen X:
    zoomCameraX = cameraX + (1 - 0.90) * playerX
- Cube vertical zoom is anchored to fixed world ground center Y=105:
    zoomCameraY = cameraY + (1 - 0.90) * 105 + 25
  Therefore jumping cannot move camera Y. Grounded cube placement is exactly
  newera15 placement +25 Cocos points, while jump height is rendered at 0.90x.
- Ship/ball/UFO use their newera15 real-camera framing first, then zoom around
  fixed screen center Y=160 and lift the viewport 25 points.

x86 purple-slider / freeze safety retained
------------------------------------------
- Horizontal Slider is not collected, disabled, retained, restored, or resynchronized.
- EditorUI::updateSlider() is not required by OLD_VER_PLAYTEST and is not called on Stop.
- Wrapper Play/Pause click is consumed by the Windows host before native touch dispatch,
  preventing the editor control underneath from entering selected state.
- Ordinary editor CCMenu controls remain suspended/restored during playtest.
