Geometry Dash Wrapper 0.9.7-newera5 verification notes

Changes from 0.9.7-newera4-fix2
--------------------------------------
1. Endless pre-1.8 inline editor playtest
   - EndPortalObject::triggerObject is required by the OLD_VER_PLAYTEST capability check.
   - x86: saves the original first byte, writes RET (C3), FlushInstructionCache is used by patch_x86_code, and restores the byte when playtest ends or the scene changes.
   - legacy ARM: saves the original Thumb/ARM entry instruction, writes Thumb BX LR (4770) or ARM BX LR (E12FFF1E), calls Dynarmic A32 Jit::ClearCache(), and restores the original instruction on teardown/scene change.
   - The existing far-ahead EndPortalObject movement is retained only as a secondary guard.

2. Preview audio teardown
   - StopInlineOldVersionPlaytest explicitly calls audio_stop_background on x86 and legacy ARM.
   - Scene-loss cleanup also stops background music.

3. Editor play button size
   - GJ_playBtn2_001.png wrapper scale changed from 0.65 to 0.325 on x86 and legacy ARM.

4. Intentional non-change
   - The short first attempt followed by the stable second attempt is not modified in newera5.

5. Build cache
   - Dynarmic builder revision: dynarmic-x64-builder115-0.9.7-newera5
