UPLOADED LOG DIAGNOSIS
----------------------
- The EnduranceTest9 run retained the working BPM setup path.
- The old background anchor captured only the primary node and restored it once.
  The user observed that the black region continued while playtesting and
  disappeared on Stop.
- Exact selected-APK disassembly contains three calls to updateCameraBGArt: one
  in LevelEditorLayer::updateEditor and one in each shared new/old camera path.
  EnduranceTest8/9 redirected only the direct editor call.
- The failing invalid-ground run ends immediately after the host reports a null
  texture. EnduranceTest9 wrote fallback blend fields but then allowed
  SpriteBatchNode::initWithTexture to continue with the invalid texture.
- The APK contains ground art 1-18 and background art 1-26, while the community
  binary's load/get routines clamp to 30 and 45 respectively.

ENDURANCETEST10 CHANGES
-----------------------
- Validates and patches GameManager::loadGround/getGTexture from max 30 to 18.
- Validates and patches GameManager::loadBackground/getBGTexture from max 45 to 26.
- Invalid community selector entries therefore resolve to the last packaged
  texture before SpriteBatchNode construction instead of producing null art.
- Redirects all three exact updateCameraBGArt callsites through a guest-side
  conditional thunk. The host suppresses the call only when `this` is the
  active LevelEditorLayer. Normal PlayLayer gameplay executes the untouched
  original function directly, without a nested-emulation call.
- Removes the ineffective background-node position anchor.
- Retains the defensive exact host updateBlendFunc implementation, but no longer
  claims a missing texture can be fully recovered after construction begins.
