-------
- ARM legacy and ARMv7 no longer abort a valid large-level Save / Save & Play
  merely because nativeTouchesEnd exceeds the old 10-second wall-clock guard.

- Other touch/input callbacks retain the existing 10-second watchdog.
- Slow nativeTouchesEnd calls taking at least 1 second log per-call import
  deltas plus guest-heap status.
- x86-native behavior is unchanged.
- No camera, playtest, restart-button, networking, editor Delete-key,
  input-recovery, save-path, or GDPS behavior was intentionally changed.
