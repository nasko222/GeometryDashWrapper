Fix1 design
-----------
- Removes CCMotionStreak from the pre-1.8 bridge entirely.
- Never reparents the real PlayerObject. The hidden PlayLayer retains ownership and remains the physics authority.
- Creates editor-side sprite proxies for the selected player icon and mirrors position/rotation/scale from the real hidden player.
- Breadcrumbs use ordinary green streak.png CCSprites under a dedicated CCNode, avoiding scheduled CCMotionStreak::update callbacks.
- Existing hidden-PlayLayer camera sync, death/retry guard, visible stop button, Escape stop, and Attempt-2 pre-present guard remain.
